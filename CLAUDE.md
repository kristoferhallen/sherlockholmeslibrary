# Kristofer's Sherlock Holmes library

Hugo site with reviews of Sherlock Holmes pastiches, live at
https://sherlock.kristoferhallen.com. The site is in English; Kristofer
writes to Claude in Swedish.

**Open work:** `docs/todo.md`. Read it when asked what is left, and tick off
or remove items when they are done.

## Publishing

- A push to `main` builds and deploys (GitHub Actions: `hugo --minify`, FTP
  upload of `public/`). Commit and push only when Kristofer asks.
- Check `git log HEAD..origin/main` before pushing; he sometimes commits
  from the GitHub web UI or another session.
- After a push, wait for `gh run watch` and check production with `curl`.
- Local preview: `.claude/launch.json` config `sherlock-hugo` (port 1317).
  Port 1313 is usually his main site (`../kristoferhallen`).

## Structure

- **Reviews:** `content/posts/<slug>/index.md` + `cover.jpg` (page bundles).
  Front matter: `title`, `book_title`, `author`, `author_last` (sorting),
  `date`, `grade` (0–10), `description`, `tags`, `cover`, and for series
  books `series`, `series_order`, `publication_year`.
  `not_a_book: true` keeps a review (the Lego book nook) out of rankings
  and home page stats.
- **Unread books:** `content/unread/<slug>/` with the same fields but no
  grade or date. Never rendered on their own; only the reading guides list
  them. To review one, move the bundle to `content/posts/`.
- **Reading guides:** `content/guides/<series>.md` with `guide_series`
  (must match the reviews' `series` exactly), `byline`, `notes`, `weight`.
  Shown as "Series" in the menu; `/series/*` redirects to the guides.
  Series descriptions: `data/series_descriptions.yaml` (keys are the
  capitalised term titles).
- **Book list:** `/books/` (by author) and `/top-books/` (by rating) are the
  same list, `layouts/partials/book-index.html` + `book-row.html`.
- **Sherlock Meets:** `data/sherlockmeets.yaml` (name, group, description,
  image, books, wikipedia). Groups and their order are in the front matter
  of `content/sherlock-meets/_index.md`. Portraits live in
  `assets/images/sherlock-meets/`. Reviews link back automatically.
- **SEO:** `<title>` from `layouts/partials/seo-title.html` (`seo_title` in
  front matter overrides it), description from `seo-description.html`,
  noindex rules in `noindex.html`.
- **`.htaccess`** is generated by Hugo from `layouts/index.htaccess.txt`;
  aliases in front matter become real 301s.

## Conventions

- Never edit `themes/PaperMod`; override in `layouts/`.
- The live stylesheet is `static/css/extended_custom.css` (and
  `static/css/guides.css` on guide pages). Bump the `?v=` number in
  `layouts/partials/extend_head.html` when changing them.
- Colours are tokens in `extended_custom.css`; dark mode is
  `:root[data-theme="dark"]` (must keep the `:root` to beat PaperMod).
- Check facts about books (series order, years, editions) against sources
  such as Open Library or the publisher. Do not guess.
- Workflows: new review, use the `new-review` skill; SEO or traffic
  questions, use the user-level `seo-review` skill.
