The Beekeeper's Apprentice was probably the first Holmes pastiche I ever read, and it helped me see that there are more people who love Sherlock Holmes and that the stories are still being written. Mary Russell is fifteen when she meets a retired beekeeper on the Sussex Downs. Over nineteen books she becomes his apprentice, his partner and eventually his wife.

Each book takes the pair somewhere new: India, Morocco, France, Ireland. Mary is a very different companion from Watson, and many times she is as good as Sherlock, or better.
