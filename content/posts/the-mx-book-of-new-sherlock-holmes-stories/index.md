This is a big series of books, launched on Kickstarter in 2015 by MX Publishing with David Marcum as editor. It started as a trilogy and ended ten years later at 52 books. Many authors have contributed, and the royalties go to Undershaw (formerly Stepping Stones School), a school for children with special educational needs housed in Sir Arthur Conan Doyle's former home in Hindhead, Surrey.

All the stories are traditional and faithful to the original canon; there are no horror or space themes here. With such a huge collection the quality varies, but there are a lot of good stories. Here you get the real story behind cases that are only mentioned in the canon, such as the giant rat of Sumatra and the disappearance of Mr. James Phillimore, the man who stepped back into his house to get his umbrella and was never seen again. If you come across any of the books, make sure to buy them.

Lee Child, creator of the Jack Reacher series, and [Nicholas Meyer](/guides/nicholas-meyer/), author of the landmark Sherlock Holmes novel [The Seven-Per-Cent Solution](/posts/the-seven-per-cent-solution/), have written forewords. Among the contributors you also find well-known Sherlockians such as [Bonnie MacBird](/posts/art-in-the-blood/) and [James Lovegrove](/posts/sherlock-holmes-the-thinking-engine/).
