London 1903, and a series of grisly murders with trophies taken and the bodies carefully arranged has people whispering that Jack the Ripper has returned. Then a new client turns up at Baker Street: Dr Jekyll, who wants Sherlock Holmes to clear his friend Edward Hyde of the crimes. To find the real killer and make London safe again Sherlock and Watson need help from Dracula and Mina (from the first book in the series)  This is the second book in The Classified Dossier, the best one so far — Klaver keeps the classic deductions but also adds horror elements without going to far from the original stories.
