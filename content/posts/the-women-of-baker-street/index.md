Mrs. Hudson falls ill and ends up in a hospital ward, and while she is there other women in the ward die. People die in hospitals but were these really natural deaths? Dr Watson also has a suspicion that all is not right. At the same time Mary Watson is out in London on a case of her own, children from the streets who disappear and there are rumours about strange children in black who are seen at night.

This is the second book in the Baker Street Inquiries series and the story is good, there are dark elements and suspense and Mrs. Hudson and Mary end up in real danger.
