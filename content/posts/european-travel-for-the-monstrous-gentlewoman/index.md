A letter from Vienna reaches the Athena Club: Lucinda Van Helsing, daughter of Professor Van Helsing, is being held in an asylum and used for her father's experiments. Mary Jekyll and her friends travel across Europe to rescue her, but they soon realize that they are being watched by the Société des Alchimistes. On the way they get help from Irene Adler, meet Dr Freud and later fight alongside Count Dracula himself.

Even more characters from other universes are introduced and we join a journey through late 19th century Europe. Here Sherlock and Watson are only supporting characters, but it's a great book with mystery and action.
