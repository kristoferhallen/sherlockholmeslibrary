Mary Russell's friend Veronica is a member of the New Temple and Veronica's friend, also a member, is murdered. Mary investigates and together with Holmes they try to solve the mystery in 1920s London. 

In the second book about Mary Russell and Sherlock Holmes, Mary is now 21 and the relationship between Mary and Sherlock is becoming something more than friendship. As with the first book in the series this was one of the earliest pastiches that I read after reading the original stories. Great story, still closer to the originals with Sherlock Holmes in London and Lestrade involved in the case.
